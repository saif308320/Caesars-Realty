Fees, in plain terms

Standard Karachi market rates — we quote the exact figure on your file before you sign anything.

Buying

1% of sale price

Paid on completion, standard across Karachi agencies. Covers shortlisting, visits and paperwork support through to transfer.

Selling

1% of sale price

Includes listing, photography, buyer screening and negotiation. No upfront fee — we're paid when the deal closes.

Renting

1 month's rent

Split between landlord and tenant per market norm, due once the lease is signed and keys change hands.

These are typical Karachi market rates and can vary by property type or deal size — your advisor confirms the final figure in writing before any commitment.
